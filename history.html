Where History Began

Xi'an, anciently known as Chang'an, served as the capital for 13 dynasties over 3,000 years. As the eastern terminus of the Silk Road, it was once the largest city in the world, welcoming traders and travelers from across Asia and Europe. Today, it stands as a living museum of Chinese civilization.
